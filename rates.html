Important Billing Notes & Additional Charge Guidelines

  • Official Portal / Statutory Fees: All rates displayed above are strictly our sample service charges in INR (₹) for computer time, printing, scanning, and operator typing assistance. Any official examination, university, or government portal fee is paid directly on the official portal at actuals via the customer's UPI/Card.
  • Specialty Paper & Heavy Ink Coverage: Standard print rates apply to 75 GSM A4 bond paper. Upgrading to 100 GSM bond adds ₹2/page; 250 GSM certificate cardstock or glossy photo media is billed at the specialty sheet rate.
  • Bulk & Institutional Discounts: Students, educators, and businesses printing 200+ pages or booking multiple workstations can request a custom discounted slab at the counter.
